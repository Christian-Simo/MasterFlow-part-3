About Master Flow

Our History

Master Flow was established in 1999. We were first located in Hatfield, which is in Pretoria. We specialise in commercial plumbing. Our commitment to the industry and our excellence in service delivery upholds our values of honesty and trust, not just within our organisation but also towards our employees, stakeholders and our customers. At Master Flow, we consistently spark innovation and upgrade our specialised services to allow us to establish new benchmarks for quality and excellence.

Our Mission Statement

At Master Flow, taking care of our team and our customers is at the heart of everything we do. We show up, do great work, and charge a fair price for it—plain and simple. We’re always looking for ways to do things better because we aren't just trying to complete a job; we want to be the plumbers you rely on for life.

Our Vision Statement

At Master Flow, the future is one where the business is known for its skills and new ideas in plumbing. The business wants to make life better for communities by doing things using new technology and offering the best service. The business always tries to be the best in the industry, making smarter and more efficient solutions for a better future.
